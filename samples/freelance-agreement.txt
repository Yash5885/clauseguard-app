FREELANCE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT
Demonstration contract. Fictional parties; not a legal template.

Harbor Creative Ltd. engages Mira Ellis, an independent designer, to develop a brand identity and six website page designs over eight weeks. Deliverables and dates are described in the approved project schedule.

1. Scope and acceptance
The fee includes two rounds of reasonable revisions. Additional work requires a written change order specifying scope, price, and timing. Customer shall review each milestone within seven business days and give specific reasons for rejection. Acceptance shall not be unreasonably withheld.

2. Payment terms
The total fee is twelve thousand pounds: twenty-five percent at kickoff, fifty percent on design approval, and the balance on delivery. Undisputed invoices are due within thirty days of receipt. Customer may withhold only genuinely disputed amounts and shall explain the dispute promptly.

3. Intellectual property
Upon full payment, Designer assigns ownership of the final approved deliverables specifically created for Customer. Designer retains pre-existing tools, methods, rejected concepts, and general know-how. Customer receives a perpetual nonexclusive license to embedded background materials only as needed to use the paid deliverables.

4. Third-party materials
Fonts, stock photographs, and open-source components remain subject to their respective licenses. Designer shall disclose third-party license requirements before incorporation. Customer is responsible for renewal fees expressly approved in writing.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect nonpublic information with reasonable care for three years after disclosure. Publicly available, previously known, independently developed, and lawfully received third-party information is excluded. Portfolio publication requires Customer written consent.

6. Liability
Each party liability is capped at total fees paid or payable, excluding fraud and willful misconduct. Neither party is liable for indirect losses. Each party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate losses arising from breach.

7. Indemnification
Designer shall defend third-party intellectual property claims concerning original deliverables, excluding claims caused by unauthorized modifications or Customer materials. Customer shall give prompt notice and reasonable cooperation. No settlement admitting fault or imposing nonmonetary duties may be made without Customer consent. Indemnification remains subject to the liability cap.

8. Termination and disputes
Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within fourteen days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience on fourteen days notice and shall pay for completed work and approved noncancelable commitments. The parties shall negotiate disputes for thirty days before attempting remote mediation with shared costs. Designer may serve other clients without disclosing Customer confidential information.
